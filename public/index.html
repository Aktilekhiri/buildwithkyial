How a project works

Four steps, the same for a one-page site or a full web app.

  1. Free call

    We talk for 20 minutes about your goals, audience, budget and timeline. Within two business days you get a fixed quote.

  2. Design

    I design the key pages in Figma. You review them and get two rounds of changes before any code is written.

  3. Build

    I build the site and send you a preview link every week, so you can click through real pages as they come together.

  4. Launch and grow

    I connect your domain, set up SEO and analytics, and record a short video showing you how to edit your site.

Questions clients ask

Don't see yours? Ask me directly.

How much does a website cost?

Landing pages start at $1,200, business websites at $2,800, Shopify stores at $3,500 and Next.js web apps at $6,000. After a free call you get a fixed quote in writing, so the price doesn't change halfway through.

How long does it take to build a website?

A landing page takes 1–2 weeks, a business website 3–4 weeks, a Shopify store 3–5 weeks and a custom Next.js app 4–8 weeks. The biggest factor is how quickly content and feedback arrive.

Do you work with clients in the United States?

Yes. I work remotely with US clients, quote and invoice in USD, sign a simple contract and schedule calls during your business hours.

How do payments work?

Usually 50% to start and 50% before launch. Larger projects are split into milestones, so you only pay for finished work. Invoices are in USD.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every site launches with technical SEO in place: fast load times, clean page structure, meta tags, schema markup, a sitemap and Google Search Console. Rankings take time and depend on your competition, so I won't promise the number one spot. I will make sure Google can find and understand your site.

Do I own the website when it's finished?

Yes. You own the code, the design files, the domain and every account. Nothing is locked to me.

Can you redesign or fix my existing website?

Yes. I can redesign your site, move it to a faster platform, fix a slow or broken Shopify store, or take over updates with a care plan.
